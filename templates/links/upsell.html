Questions

How is a booking request credited to a link?

When someone opens your link, we remember it on their device. If they send you a booking request on Island Stay within 14 days, it counts for that link.

Where does the link take people?

To your Island Stay page, or straight to the booking request form if you choose. Guests still contact you directly.

What if I stop paying?

Your links keep opening your page, so nothing you've printed or posted breaks. We just stop counting visits until you renew.

Is visitor data private?

Yes. We count visits and record the device type and the website a visitor came from. We don't store IP addresses or show you anything that identifies a visitor.

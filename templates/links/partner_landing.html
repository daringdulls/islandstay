Questions

Who pays me?

The business pays you directly, for example by bank transfer, as set out in its terms. Island Stay tracks your links and keeps the record of every commission, but doesn't handle payments.

When is a booking credited to me?

When a guest opens your link and sends that business a booking request within 14 days. If they used another partner's link more recently, the most recent one counts.

When do I earn the commission?

When the business confirms the booking it shows as pending. After the guest has stayed, the business approves it, then pays you and marks it paid. Cancelled bookings don't earn commission.

What do I see about guests?

Only a first name and initial, the dates and the booking value. Never their contact details.

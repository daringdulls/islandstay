How to use it

  • Newest post is top left, just like the profile. Tiles with a dashed outline are in {{ month|date:"F" }}.
  • Drag a tile onto another to swap their dates. The calendar updates too.
  • The plan alternates photo and solid-colour tiles, so the grid reads as a checkerboard.
  • Click a tile to download the image and copy the caption. Reels and Stories have a shot list or sticker note.
  • Schedule each one in Meta Business Suite (Planner → Create post / reel / story), then mark it Scheduled.

Ways to partner

{% icon "map-pin" %} Sponsor island guides

A clearly labelled card on the island pages you choose, for example every island in an atoll, or the busiest guest house islands. Ideal for island councils, dive and excursion brands, resorts offering day trips, and ferry or seaplane operators.

{% icon "book-open" %} Sponsor travel guides

Appear beside the guides travellers read before they book: getting around, money and SIM cards, local rules, diving and marine life.

{% icon "signal" %} Affiliate partnerships

Travel insurance, eSIMs, flights, airport transfers and tours in our 'Before you go' box, paid on results.

{% icon "camera" %} Social media

A post or Story on @islandstaymv, made with our own design templates and clearly marked as a partnership.

How we work

  • Every placement is labelled 'Sponsored', and links are marked as sponsored for search engines.
  • You get a report of views and clicks for your campaign.
  • We only accept partners that are useful to travellers and respect local islands and their rules.
  • Sponsorship never affects which hosts we list or verify.

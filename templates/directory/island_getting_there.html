Tips for the transfer day

  • Send your flight number to your host so they can book you on the right speedboat.
  • Speedboats stop in the evening. If you land late, spend a night in Hulhumalé next to the airport.
  • Boats can be delayed by weather. Leave a buffer before your flight home.

{# ---------- seasons ---------- #}
When to go

The reef has seasons too

{% icon "waves" %}

Manta rays

Hanifaru Bay in Baa is famous from roughly May to November. Ari's cleaning stations are busier from about December to April.

{% icon "fish" %}

Whale sharks

Seen all year in the South Ari Marine Protected Area, around Dhigurah and Dhangethi.

{% icon "sun" %}

Calm, clear water

The dry season, about December to April, usually brings flat seas and the best visibility.

{% icon "sailboat" %}

Surf

Waves are most consistent from about March to October, around North Malé breaks such as Thulusdhoo and Himmafushi.

Seasons vary year to year. Your host or operator will know what's being seen this week.

{# ---------- etiquette + CTA ---------- #}
Good to know

On the reef

  • {% icon "leaf" %}Don't touch or stand on coral. It's alive, and it's what feeds the island.
  • {% icon "turtle" %}Give wildlife space: stay a few metres from whale sharks, mantas and turtles, and never chase or touch them.
  • {% icon "droplets" %}Use reef-safe sunscreen, or a rash vest.
  • {% icon "shield-check" %}Go with a licensed operator: look for the Verified badge, and ask about life jackets and first aid.
  • {% icon "life-buoy" %}Diving before your flight home? Leave about 24 hours between your last dive and take-off.

Why a guest house

What staying on a local island is like

{% icon "heart-handshake" %}

Hosted by a family

Your host knows the boat crews, the best snorkel spots and who cooks the best fish on the island.

{% icon "banknote" %}

Fair prices

Rooms cost a fraction of a resort, and you pay the host directly with no booking fees on top.

{% icon "umbrella" %}

Beach and reef close by

Most guest houses are a short walk from the beach, and many islands have a bikini beach for visitors.

{% icon "landmark" %}

Real island life

Mosques, schools, cafés and harbours. Dress modestly in the village, and enjoy being a guest.

{# ---------- tips + CTA ---------- #}
Before you book

Good questions to ask your host

  • {% icon "plane-landing" %}Transfer: can they arrange the boat from your flight, and what does it cost?
  • {% icon "coffee" %}Meals: is breakfast included, and can they cook dinner?
  • {% icon "umbrella" %}Beach: how far is the bikini beach from the guest house?
  • {% icon "sailboat" %}Trips: which excursions do they run, and on which days?
  • {% icon "banknote" %}Payment: cash or card, and in US dollars or rufiyaa?

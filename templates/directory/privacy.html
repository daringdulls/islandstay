Our legal basis

  • Your request: to take the steps you asked for before a possible booking.
  • Analytics cookies: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using Cookie settings.
  • Business accounts: our agreement with you to list your business.
  • Security and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

Who we share it with

  • The business you contact receives your request. When you send it on WhatsApp, Viber, email or phone, that service's own privacy terms apply.
  • Service providers that run the website for us: hosting and database (Vercel, Neon) and file storage (Vercel Blob). They process data only on our instructions.

We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising, and we don't use third-party trackers.

How long we keep it

  • Booking requests: up to 2 years, so you and the business can refer back to them.
  • Analytics: deleted automatically after 13 months.
  • Business accounts: while the account is open, then up to 1 year.

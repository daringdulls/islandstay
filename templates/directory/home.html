How Island Stay works

No booking engine, no middleman. A real conversation leads to a real island stay.

  1. {% icon "map" %}Find your island

    Each guide covers the beaches, local rules, facilities and how to get there from the airport.

  2. {% icon "message-circle" %}Message the host

    Send a request with your dates. The host replies on WhatsApp, Viber or email, usually within a day.

  3. {% icon "heart-handshake" %}Agree and stay

    Agree the price and the transfer with your host. You pay them directly: no booking fees, ever.

{# ---------- FAQ ---------- #}
Questions

Good to know

Anything else? How requests work explains the guest side.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. Listing is free and we take no commission. Guests pay you directly, however you agree.

Do I need a booking system or calendar?

No. Guests send a request with their dates, and you reply to confirm availability and price. There's nothing to keep in sync.

How do guests reach me?

On WhatsApp, Viber, email or phone, whichever you add. Each request is also saved in your dashboard so nothing gets lost.

How do I get the Verified host badge?

Upload your tourism licence or business registration from your dashboard. We check it and add the cowrie badge, usually within a few days.

Can I list more than one business?

Yes. Add as many listings as you run, for example a guest house and an excursion service, all from one account.

My island isn't on the site yet.

Tell us with Suggest an island. We're adding guides every week ({{ islands_count }} so far).

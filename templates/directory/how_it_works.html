How {{ SITE_NAME }} works

We're a local directory, not a booking engine. We connect you directly with the people who run the guest houses, resorts and tours on each island.

For travellers

  1. Explore an island's guide: what it's famous for, what to expect, activities and how to get there.
  2. Pick a place to stay or a service on that island.
  3. Send a request with your dates, group size and questions. It's emailed straight to the owner, and you get a copy with a reference number.
  4. The owner replies to you directly — by email, WhatsApp or phone, whichever you chose — to confirm availability, the final price and how to pay.

We never take payments or card details, and sending a request doesn't commit you to anything.

For businesses

Create a free account, add your listing under your island and wait for our team to approve it. Requests are emailed to you and saved in your dashboard, where you can track each one as replied, confirmed or declined.

Businesses with this badge have shown our team a valid Ministry of Tourism licence or business registration. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, but verification is an extra check. As with any travel arrangement, agree prices and payment terms in writing with the host.

Staying on a local island

  • Local islands are home to Maldivian communities, and Islam is the state religion. Dress modestly in the village: cover your shoulders and knees.
  • Swimwear is fine on designated bikini beaches, on excursions and on resort day trips.
  • Alcohol isn't sold on local islands. Resorts, liveaboards and some floating bars serve it.
  • Friday is the weekly day of rest: public ferries run less and shops close around midday prayers.
  • Transfers are by speedboat, public ferry, seaplane or domestic flight. Your host can usually arrange them for you.

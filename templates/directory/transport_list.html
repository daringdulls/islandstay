Transport tips

  • Speedboats are timed around international flights; tell your host your flight number.
  • Public ferries are the cheapest option but don't run on Fridays.
  • Domestic flights have lower baggage allowances (often 20 kg) — check before you fly.
  • Rough seas (May–October) can delay boats; plan a buffer before your flight home.
